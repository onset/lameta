## Improvements

- The Problems panel
  - lameta now checks every project for a session that has no files to export, a session that has no contributors, a language that sessions call by different names, and a contributor who has no consent file. Before, only ELAR projects got these checks.
  - The top of the Problems panel now says which checks are running. It says "ELAR Checks" when your archive adds checks of its own, and "Baseline Checks" when lameta runs only its own.
  - Translators can now translate every message from ELAR's checks into your interface language.
  - The Problems panel now lists a session whose IMDI lameta can't generate, and clicking it takes you to the session.
- The IMDI tab no longer shows lameta's own comments.

## Fixes

- lameta no longer closes the project when the folder of a session or a person disappears while the project is open, as when OneDrive applies a rename that someone made on another computer. lameta tells you once that it can't save that folder, and you can keep working on the rest of the project.
