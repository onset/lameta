## Improvements

- lameta now checks your metadata against your archive's rules while you work, and again when you export. A Problems panel lists anything that the archive would reject. Click a problem to go to its field. ELAR is the first archive whose rules lameta checks.
- lameta now works better with projects that OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive or a similar service keeps in sync.
- Updated translations of lameta's interface.
